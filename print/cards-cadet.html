StS Sentinel · System 09 · The Bridge · Cadet

Re-entry angle

The Sentinel's heat shield is safe between 5.2° and 7.2°. Steeper than 7.2° and the ship burns up. Shallower than 5.2° and it skips off the air back into space. Navigation has three paths. Path A goes in at 2°. Path B goes in at 6°. Path C goes in at 12°. Pick the safe path and type its angle into the ship computer.

The last crew's notes

No pulleys, cups or rubber bands? A crew before you wrote down what they saw. Use their notes for these stations.
Engine lift: pulled 8 cm of rope, the engine rose 2 cm. Pulled 20 cm, it rose 5 cm. Pulled 32 cm, it rose 8 cm.
Engine gears: no gears? Use the paper gears. The big gear has 48 teeth and the small one has 12. Each turn of the small gear moves the big gear along by 12 teeth.
Fuel tank: after 1 cup the tank was one fifth full. After 2 cups it was two fifths full.
Fuel balance: 4 coins at 4 cm, the left side went down. At 6 cm, the ruler was level. At 8 cm, the right side went down.
Navigation wiring: no kit? Trace each path on the diagram with a pencil, from the battery and back. A lamp lights only if your pencil gets all the way around without crossing a gap.
Thrusters: with 1 rubber band the tray slid 40 cm. With 2 it slid 90 cm. With 3 it slid 150 cm.
