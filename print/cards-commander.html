StS Sentinel · System 09 · The Bridge · Commander

Re-entry angle

The Sentinel's heat shield is safe between 5.2° and 7.2°. Steeper than 7.2° and the ship burns up. Shallower than 5.2° and it skips off the air back into space. The ship moves at 11,000 m/s along its path, and each path has its own speed straight down. The sine of the angle below level is the speed down ÷ the speed along the path. Path A: 350 m/s down. Path B: 1,150 m/s down. Path C: 2,300 m/s down. Work out all three, pick the safe one, and type its angle.

StS Sentinel · One per crew · Commander

Formula card

Every formula this mission needs. Leave out friction and air everywhere except in the re-entry.
Work: W = F × d (joules = newtons × meters)
Torque: τ = F × r. Meshed gears push with the same F, so τ grows with r (and with the number of teeth).
Boyle's law, same temperature: P1 × V1 = P2 × V2
Ohm's law: V = I × R. Lamps one after another add: R = R1 + R2.
Momentum: p = m × v. When something pushes off, the two momenta are equal and opposite.
The angle below level: sin(angle) = speed down ÷ speed along the path
