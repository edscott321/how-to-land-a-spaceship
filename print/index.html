How to Land a Spaceship: what to print

Every page is US Letter and black and white. Print from the PDF, or open a page and print it from the browser. The words on the cards are the same as in the teacher guide.

Everything in one PDFOpen the ship computer

The one PDF is the Officer level (grades 6 to 9). For Cadet or Commander, print it too and swap in that level's crew cards. The signs, wiring diagram, rope drawing, gears, log, map and certificate are the same at every level.

Printing tips

In Chrome's print window, leave Margins on Default and Scale at 100, and if the grid on the room map is missing, turn on Background graphics. The gears must print at 100% (in a PDF reader, choose Actual size); a bar on the page tells you if they didn't.

Using 3 pieces of rope instead of 4? The engine code becomes 40 and the cipher shift becomes 3 (both on the ship computer's setup screen). If you also use the paper main computer card, write the message moved 3 places instead: HQWUB VSHHG HOHYHQ WKRXVDQG.
