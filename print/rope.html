StS Sentinel · Engine Room · System 02 · Engine lift

Rig the rope like this

Tie one end to the left eyehole. Go down under the first track of the engine's pulley, up over the big pulley, down under the second track, then up through the right eyehole. Pull the free end.

4 pieces of rope hold the engine. The pieces share the weight.How to Land a Spaceship
